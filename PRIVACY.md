# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 22, 2026**

> **TL;DR:** We only know your public wallet address. We cannot see your balance, your keys, or anything happening inside a game. We don't sell data. One session cookie. That's it.

---

## 1. Who We Are

Calstin ("we", "us", "our") is a Web3 game launcher that lets players discover and launch blockchain-enabled games from a single interface. Our platform is operated at [calstin.com](https://calstin.com).

---

## 2. What Information We Collect

### Wallet Address
When you sign in with a crypto wallet (e.g. Phantom, MetaMask), we receive your public wallet address. This is a public identifier on the blockchain — it is not private information. We never receive your private key or seed phrase.

### Authentication Data
To verify you own a wallet, we ask you to sign a plain-text message. This signature is verified server-side and then discarded. No on-chain transaction is made and no gas is consumed.

### Usage Data
We collect anonymised logs of which pages you visit and which games you launch. We do not record in-game activity, wallet balances, transaction history, or anything that happens inside a game iframe.

### Local Storage
We use browser localStorage to store your session token, UI preferences (theme, layout), and game playtime counters. This data never leaves your device unless you sign in, at which point the session token is sent with API requests.

---

## 3. What We Do NOT Collect

- Private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords
- In-game signing requests or transaction data
- Wallet balances or token holdings
- Financial information of any kind
- Third-party game data — games run in isolated iframes and are subject to their own privacy policies

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information

- To authenticate your session and keep you signed in
- To display your profile, achievements, and playtime stats
- To improve platform performance and fix bugs
- To send important security alerts (if you opt in)

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your wallet address or usage data with advertisers. We may share anonymised, aggregated statistics (e.g. "most-played game this week") publicly. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

---

## 6. Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in. It is flagged HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Strict. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies.

---

## 7. Third-Party Games

Games launched through Calstin run inside cross-origin iframes on the game developer's own domain. By the browser's Same-Origin Policy, we cannot access anything inside those iframes. Each game is governed by its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for data collected by third-party games.

---

## 8. Data Retention

Session tokens expire after 30 days of inactivity. Anonymised usage logs are retained for up to 12 months for performance analysis. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing [contact — coming soon].

---

## 9. Your Rights

- **Access:** request a copy of the data we hold about you
- **Correction:** ask us to fix inaccurate data
- **Deletion:** ask us to delete your account and associated data
- **Portability:** receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Objection:** opt out of any processing you disagree with

---

## 10. Security

All data is transmitted over TLS. Security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy) are enforced on every response. Our security posture is publicly documented — see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in-app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

---

## 12. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, use the contact form at calstin.com. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
